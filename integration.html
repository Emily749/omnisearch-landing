Integration Guide

Adding OmniSearch to your store takes minutes.

  1. Copy the script snippet provided upon pilot approval.
  2. Paste it into the <head> section of your store's theme.
  3. OmniSearch automatically scans your product pages and renders safety badges next to each item.

Platform Notes

Shopify: paste the snippet into theme.liquid, above the closing body tag.

WooCommerce: paste the snippet into your theme's footer.php file, or add through a header and footer plugin.

Custom sites: paste the snippet into your root layout or shared footer component.
